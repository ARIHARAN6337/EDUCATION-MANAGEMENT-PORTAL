❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I access the student portal dashboard?

Click on the 'Student / Staff / Admin Login' button at the top right, select 'Student Portal', and log in using your college assigned ID and password.

What are the eligibility criteria for engineering admissions?

Candidates must have passed 10+2 higher secondary examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with a minimum aggregate of 50%.

How can I download my semester fee receipts and ID card?

Log into your respective portal account and navigate to the profile or finance section to instantly download verified digital copies.
